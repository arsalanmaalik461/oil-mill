# Privacy Policy — Namat Oil Mill

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Namat Oil Mill is a business ledger (khata) app used by a business owner and
their authorised staff to keep their own business records.

## Information the app handles

- **Business records you enter** — party (customer/supplier) names, phone
  numbers, ledger entries, bills, stock, cash and expense records. These are
  stored on your device and, when you sign in with your app account, synced to
  Google Firebase (Firestore) under your own account so your data is backed
  up and available on your own phones.
- **Google Drive (optional)** — if you connect a Google account in Settings,
  the app uploads backup files and ledger PDFs to **your own** Google Drive.
  The app can only access files it created itself.
- **Contacts (optional)** — if you grant the contacts permission and use the
  "fill party numbers" feature, the app reads contact names and numbers **on
  your device only** to fill in party phone numbers. Contacts are never
  uploaded or shared.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
- The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Data retention and deletion

Your records stay in your account until you delete them. Uninstalling the app
removes the data stored on that device. Cloud data for an account can be
deleted on request by the account holder.
